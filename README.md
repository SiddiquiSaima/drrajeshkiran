4. Patient Education / Doctor Instructions Section
Create a section where the doctor can guide patients with important kidney health instructions.

Title:
Kidney Health Guidance

Include cards:
- When should you consult a nephrologist?
  Swelling in legs or face, high creatinine, protein in urine, blood in urine, uncontrolled BP, reduced urine output, diabetes with kidney issues.

- Before your appointment
  Bring latest blood reports, urine reports, ultrasound reports, current medicines, discharge summary if available, and previous kidney-related records.

- Dialysis patient guidance
  Track weight, BP, urine output, dialysis schedule, and report symptoms like breathlessness, swelling, fever, or weakness.

- Transplant patient guidance
  Do not miss medicines, monitor creatinine regularly, avoid self-medication, and consult immediately if fever or reduced urine output occurs.

- Kidney stone prevention guidance
  Maintain hydration, follow diet advice, avoid self-medication, and get evaluated if pain, fever, or blood in urine occurs.

Add a note:
This information is for general awareness only and does not replace direct medical consultation.
